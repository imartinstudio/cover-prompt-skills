## Extracted style notes

The post presents a reusable “Anthropic Research” cover language built around restraint rather than decorative complexity:

- a large, mostly flat background color field;
- a high-contrast serif title with a book or research-report character;
- low-saturation color systems that can change by topic without becoming random;
- a sparse abstract hand-drawn line illustration used as a metaphor;
- a small number of irregular organic, paper, circular, or rectangular accents;
- a single title as the visual focus, with generous breathing room;
- no marketing-banner treatment, dense infographic, cyberpunk, neon, gradient, glossy 3D, or official brand mark.
